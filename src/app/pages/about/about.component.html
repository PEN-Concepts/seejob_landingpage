Our History
I've been a general contractor for over 30 years. See Job Run started because I was tired of losing my notes and struggled with keeping track of job schedules.

2022

Have you ever asked yourself . . . "1 Notepad for 1 Job or 1 Notepad for all my jobs?" Ever grabbed a 2x4 to write a lumber list or note?
Those of us who struggle with a little ADHD know what I'm talking about. Out of site . . . Out of mind. Missing appointments, behind on jobs because you have too many hats to where. Subs not knowing what is in your head and you don't have a way to keep everyone on the same page together? For me Pen & Paper wasn't going to fix that. So I built something that would.

2022–2025

I created See Job Run, one feature at a time, the same way you build a project. Scheduling, job tracking, leads, digital notepads, job calendars, none of it made the cut just because it "looked good". Everything works together to make all your jobs flow smoothly.

2026

This year I optimized everything — full Dark Mode, more features, tough Spartan look, and a personalized Spartan Dashboard to further balance your Work/Life.

Continuous improvements based on your feedback.
